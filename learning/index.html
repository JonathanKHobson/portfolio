THE THREAD THROUGH MY WORK

Listen closely.
Make room to try.

A good question can change
the whole story.

I came to learning design through documentary storytelling, technical communication, and UX. Each asks me to notice what someone is trying to understand and where the explanation stops helping.

That question follows me into the work: a beginner comparing two AI drafts, a team sketching a memory they want to preserve, or a mentor deciding whether a suggested task fits a learner. I like turning an abstract discussion into something people can look at, try, and question together.

Outside this work, I run tabletop games. The setting is different, but the attention is familiar: explain enough to begin, listen to what the group does, and adapt the structure around the people in it.

How I build learning with others

Concrete practice, room for critique, and an honest account of what was evaluated.

01 What changed after a reviewer challenged your course?

The review found choices that were too easy to eliminate and feedback that revealed an answer. I revised the distractors, added application to a definition question, and pointed retry feedback toward the relevant task. The documented revision shows my response to critique; improved learner performance still needs measurement.

See the reviewer finding and my revision
02 How do you facilitate without taking over the ideas?

I give people a sequence and something concrete to work on. For KeepMake, I designed and circulated the activity plan, then helped document the workshop output and synthesis. The sketches and directions were collaborative. My contribution was structuring the work and helping the group carry it forward.

See the workshop plan and shared outputs
03 How would you check that learning transfers?

I would ask people to apply the same judgment to a new task, explain their choice, and revise after feedback. For the AI course, that means a fresh content request rather than repeating the demonstrated prompt. This is the next evaluation I would run; the existing course record does not establish a transfer result.
