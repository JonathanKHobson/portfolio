IN PRACTICE · ARIZONA STATE UNIVERSITY

Put the mentor’s judgment into the workflow.

As an AI Analyst at ASU Learning Innovations in 2024, I proposed a staged workflow for personalized learner tasks. A mentor would validate the learner context, then review or revise generated tasks before they reached the learner. The proposal made those review points part of the process.

My route into AI runs through documentary storytelling, communication, learning design, and UX research. Each taught me to ask who the work is for, what they are trying to do, and what would help them move forward.

As an AI Analyst at Arizona State University, I worked on conversational experiences and proposed learner-support workflows. I now bring that same interest in human judgment to product design, independent AI tools, and teaching. I also run tabletop games: a useful reminder that a good system still needs someone paying attention to the people in it.
