IN PRACTICE · ARIZONA STATE UNIVERSITY

Put the mentor’s judgment into the workflow.

As an AI Analyst at ASU Learning Innovations in 2024, I proposed a staged workflow for personalized learner tasks. A mentor would validate the learner context, then review or revise generated tasks before they reached the learner. The proposal made those review points part of the process.

Proposed workflow · 2024

  1. 01

    Learner context

    Summarize the information that shapes the task.

  2. 02

    Mentor validates

    Check the context before generating.

  3. 03

    Generate & refine

    Develop a task from the reviewed context.

  4. 04

    Mentor reviews

    Confirm or revise before the learner receives it.

My route into AI runs through documentary filmmaking, learning design, UX research, and game mastering. The common thread is making something other people can enter, understand, and use.

I work with AI as a design and development partner. I bring the question, the context, and the judgment. A reusable skill or a clear context packet helps the next iteration start with what we learned, instead of repeating the same guess.

How I make AI work inspectable

Clear requirements, visible limits, and a person who can judge the result.

01 What do you test when a prompt changes?

Whether the original requirements survive. A Prompt Compass fixture catches a rewrite that drops four required field names. That check inspects prompt text; it does not prove a model will return valid JSON. I keep the next output-level test explicit instead of presenting a heuristic result as end-to-end reliability.

Inspect the regression fixture
02 How do you work with people who need to trust the answer?

I make the review point part of the workflow. My ASU learner-support proposal gives a mentor responsibility for validating learner context and reviewing generated tasks. The retained design shows where that judgment belongs and what the mentor can revise. It remains a proposal.

See the mentor review points
03 How do you adapt to incomplete evidence?

I make missing coverage visible before asking for a conclusion. Research Compass distinguishes source records, retrieved evidence and the next research question. It gives the reviewer a way to inspect the archive and decide what to investigate next; source coverage and summary accuracy still need human review.
