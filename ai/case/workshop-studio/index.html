AI Workshop Studio / LEARNING PRODUCT · AI ENABLEMENT

Make AI practice easier to follow.

A learning platform that connects instruction, practice, and review, with a lesson workspace people can navigate in several ways.

THE CHALLENGE

A video or a long prompt is only one part of learning. People also need a clear goal, a place to practice, and a way to return to the part they missed.

MY CONTRIBUTION

I connect the instructional design to the product: learner steps, supporting materials, and the interaction details that make a lesson usable.

01Give each step a purpose.02Let people find their own way back.03Design the learning journey across views.+Where it landed

01 / Decision

Give each step a purpose.

The learner view keeps the immediate goal, practice, and progress together. The course stays beside the AI tool so someone can read, try, and return.

The decision

Scaffolding means providing enough support for the next action, then giving the learner room to do it.

02 / Decision

Let people find their own way back.

I refined the media workspace around four clear destinations: Overview, Transcript, Lesson Guide, and Materials. Chapter times and transcript actions help someone return to a specific moment.

The decision

A real test caught text selection triggering a seek. Separating selection from the time control made the behavior more predictable.

03 / Workflow

Design the learning journey across views.

The platform also has learner, presenter, and audience contexts. A local rehearsal exercised how responses move through review and reveal, so a facilitator can decide what becomes shared.

Workflow, explained

  1. 01

    Learn

    The learner works on the task.

  2. 02

    Review

    The facilitator checks the response.

  3. 03

    Reveal

    The audience sees the selected contribution.

The decision

The three-view journey was locally rehearsed. That evidence is separate from hosted acceptance of every facilitation path.

WHERE IT LANDED

Something concrete to inspect.

Workshop Studio is published. The September 26 app release added the four-tab media workspace, with hosted playback and transcript seeking checked. The new ChatGPT Desktop course is still being developed.

What I learned and would test next

A saved lesson is not the same as a usable learning experience. I check playback, text selection, navigation, and the next action together.

Next, complete and review the new course with learners before making claims about learning gains.
