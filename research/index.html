THE THREAD THROUGH MY WORK

Before I studied products,
I studied stories.

A good question can change
the whole story.

Documentary filmmaking taught me to stay curious when someone’s experience did not fit the story I expected. That habit still guides my research: listen carefully, look for the exception, and be willing to change the question.

I moved through communication and learning design into UX research because I wanted those observations to shape what people could do next. Today I work across product research and design, and volunteer as a UX Research Lead at Oppia. Outside work, I facilitate tabletop games—another place where good questions and room for quieter voices matter.
