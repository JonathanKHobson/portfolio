THE THREAD THROUGH MY WORK

I started by listening
to people’s stories.

A good question can change
the whole story.

Documentary filmmaking taught me to listen closely, ask questions, and find the thread in a complicated story. I bring those habits to UX research and product design.

I still start with the person trying to make sense of something. Then I sketch options and work through the details with the team. Away from the screen, I run tabletop games, where listening, pacing, and helping a group find its way matter too.
